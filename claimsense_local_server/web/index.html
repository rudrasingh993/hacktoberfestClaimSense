02 / Inquiries

FAQ

What is proportionate deduction?

If your room costs more than the policy limit, many insurers cut other eligible charges in the same ratio (limit ÷ room rent). Check whether your policy applies it.

Is this the final claim amount?

No. It is an estimate. Your insurer's decision and your policy wording always apply.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Calculation happens in your browser.
